## About (long, for profiles and the press kit, 109 words)
Kalpa Labs is a one-person company in Hyderabad, run by Akhil. We are named after the Kalpavruksha, the wish-fulfilling tree. One tree grows seven branches. Each branch is a small tool built to help people do everyday work without confusion: a WhatsApp booking bot for salons, a job-card app for technicians, exam and tax kits, a Chrome extension that saves your browser tabs, a deals site with calculators, an AI cost tracker for software teams, and a paper-trading research bot. Our colours are leaf green, bark brown and fruit gold. We build with free tools only. Nothing here promises earnings. Everything is built and tested before it is sold.

## Rules for every Kalpavruksha line
- Plain words. No buzzwords (seamless, supercharge, revolutionize), no em dashes in sentences, no income promises (vibecheck enforces the first two).
- "Seven" means the seven product worlds b1–b7; HQ is the trunk, not a branch.
- Telugu: simple spoken words, not heavy Sanskrit. Rejected draft lines: "ఎవరైనా పండ్లు" (ungrammatical) and "కడలి పండ్లు" (కడలి = sea).
- The tree, never a temple or a deity: Kalpavruksha is the brand story, not a religious claim.
