## ✅ Setup Checklist  *(5 steps max)*  

1. **Channel‑on‑board** – activate the **WhatsApp Cloud API webhook** (`core/functions/whatsapp.js`), add the salon’s **website widget** (`web/widget.js`) and **Telegram bot** (`telegram.js`). Verify the Meta‑approved utility templates (booking‑confirm, reminder).  
2. **Secret‑seed** – create a **dashboard_token** for the owner, store it in the `clients` table, and push the salon’s Meta access token into **Supabase Vault** (read‑only RPC A51).  
3. **Data‑lock** – populate the **services, staff, hours** rows in Supabase (`bookings`, `members`). Run the **capacity‑lock script** (`core/engine.js`) to prevent double‑booking.  
4. **Owner UI** – send the owner the **dashboard URL** (`web/dashboard.html`) and the **shared‑inbox link** (`inbox.html`). Walk‑through today’s bookings view and the **30‑day report** export.  
5. **Automation‑pulse** – enable the **reminder jobs** (`core/functions/reminder.js`) and the **day‑close report**. Test a dummy booking to see the 24 h + 2 h reminders fire.

## 📊 What to Measure  *(5 KPIs)*  

- **Bookings by Bot** – total confirmed slots (dashboard → `bookings`).  
- **Reply Speed** – average time from customer message to bot reply (log `messages`).  
- **Hand‑Over Count** – how many chats escalated to a human (owner alerts).  
- **Reminder Success** – % of scheduled reminders actually sent (automation logs).  
- **Owner Engagement** – number of times the owner opened the dashboard or inbox.  

> *All metrics are pulled automatically; no manual tally needed.*

## 💰 How to Ask for the Paid Switch  

1. **Show the proof** – screen‑share the **booking tally** (≥5 real bookings) and the **reminder‑driven no‑show drop** (UNVERIFIED but observed).  
2. **Quote the offer** – “After the free 14‑day pilot, the next step is **₹2,999 setup + ₹1,499 / month** (months 1‑3) as outlined in `OFFER.md`.”  
3. **Address the fear** – re‑state the **fixed‑price safety net** (“no surprise message costs; reminders are included”).  
4. **Ask directly** – “Shall we lock in the paid plan today so you keep the momentum?”  
5. **Close with consent** – send the **DPDP‑compliant agreement** (`docs/sales/agreement.pdf`) and the **privacy notice** (`privacy-notice.pdf`) for signature.  

> *If the owner hesitates, offer the **referral‑free‑month** incentive (one extra free month per new salon they bring).*
