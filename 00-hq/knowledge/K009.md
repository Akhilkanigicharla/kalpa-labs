### ❓ FAQ (English)  

**Q: Does Tab Lifeboat sync my tabs to the cloud?**  
A: No. All backups stay on your device or in *your* Google Drive – we never see them.  

**Q: Will the extension work after I reinstall Chrome?**  
A: Yes. Export a backup file (Free) or use the Drive backup (Pro) and restore with a single click.  

**Q: Can I use Tab Lifeboat on multiple computers?**  
A: The Pro licence works on up to 3 devices. Free features work on any number of devices.  

**Q: Is there a subscription?**  
A: No. You pay a one‑time licence (yearly or lifetime). No auto‑renewals.  

**Q: What if I lose my licence key?**  
A: Keys are stored in your Chrome profile. You can also retrieve them from the purchase receipt emailed by Dodo Payments.
