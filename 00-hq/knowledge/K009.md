## 2️⃣ Full description  

### English  

> **Your tabs, backed up automatically. Restore any day, on any device. We never see them.**  

Tab Lifeboat is a lightweight tab‑ and session‑saver built with only Chrome’s official APIs. It solves the biggest pain point of OneTab, Session Buddy and similar tools – **lost tabs**.  

#### ✅ Free forever  
* Save all tabs, windows and tab‑groups in one click.  
* Restore with **exact group names and colours**.  
* Import sessions from **OneTab** and **Session Buddy**.  
* Manual export / import of backup files (JSON).  

#### 🚀 Pro (one‑time licence)  
* **Automatic snapshots** every 5 minutes – keep the last 90 days.  
* Daily backup to **your own Google Drive** (or Downloads folder).  
* One‑click restore on a new PC or after a reinstall.  
* Duplicate‑tab detection, stale‑tab cleanup and a “where did this tab go?” timeline.  

All data stays **local to your device** (or your Drive) – the extension never reads or stores your URLs on any server.  

#### Pricing (real from the plan)  
* **Pro Yearly** – $12 / ₹499 (one‑year licence key).  
* **Pro Lifetime** – $29 / ₹1 199 (up to 3 devices).  
* **Founding Lifetime** – $19 / ₹899 (first 50 buyers, limited edition).  

No subscription, no hidden fees. Purchase is handled by Dodo Payments; licence verification is done via a free public endpoint – **zero server cost** for us.  

#### Why choose Tab Lifeboat?  
| Feature | Tab Lifeboat | OneTab / Session Buddy | Workona |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unlimited tabs & groups | ✅ | ✅ (but loses groups) | ✅ (paid) |
| Automatic 90‑day history | ✅ (Pro) | ❌ | ✅ (Pro, opaque) |
| Drive‑only backup (you own the data) | ✅ (Pro) | ❌ | ❌ (their cloud) |
| One‑click import from competitors | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| No account required | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ |

Give your browsing sessions the safety net they deserve. Install Tab Lifeboat now and never scramble for lost tabs again!

## 4️⃣ FAQ  

### English  

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **What does the free version include?** | Unlimited manual saves, restore with group names/colours, OneTab & Session Buddy import, and manual JSON export/import. |
| **What do I get with Pro?** | Automatic snapshots every 5 min (90‑day history), daily backup to your Google Drive or Downloads folder, duplicate‑tab cleanup, timeline view, and one‑click restore on a new device. |
| **Is any of my browsing data sent to a server?** | No. All data stays on your device or in your own Google Drive. The licence check uses a public Dodo endpoint only. |
| **How do I purchase a licence?** | Click “Upgrade” in the extension popup, you’ll be taken to Dodo Payments. After payment you receive a licence key to paste in the settings. |
| **Can I use the same licence on multiple computers?** | The Lifetime licence works on up to 3 devices. The yearly licence is per‑device. |
| **What happens if I uninstall the extension?** | Your saved sessions remain in the backup file (or Drive). Re‑install and import the file to restore. |
| **Do I need a Google account for the free version?** | No. Only the Pro automatic‑Drive backup requires you to grant the `drive.file` permission. |
| **Is there a refund policy?** | Refunds are handled by Dodo Payments per their standard policy (see their website). |
