### 4. Quick FAQ (max 5 items)  

1. **Do I need an account?** – No. All data stays on your device or your own Google Drive.  
2. **Will the licence ever expire?** – Yearly licences are valid for 365 days; lifetime licences never expire.  
3. **Can I switch from yearly to lifetime later?** – Yes – just purchase the lifetime licence; the yearly licence is then superseded.  
4. **What payment methods are accepted?** – International cards & Indian UPI/RuPay via Dodo Payments (fees covered in margin calculations).  
5. **How do I get a refund?** – Contact support within 14 days; Dodo processes refunds (fee ≈ $1).
