## 1️⃣ Job‑Fit Checklist (10 points)  

**Part A – Core Fit (5 items)**  

| # | Check | How to verify |
|---|-------|---------------|
| 1 | Required technical skills match your résumé | Highlight each skill in the posting and tick it off if it appears in your CV |
| 2 | Minimum experience level (years / projects) satisfied | Count relevant projects or internships; ≥ required years |
| 3 | Education / certification requirement met | List degree / cert. and compare with posting |
| 4 | Location / remote‑work policy compatible | Confirm city, time‑zone, or remote eligibility |
| 5 | Salary / compensation range acceptable | Compare posting range with your expectations (if disclosed) |

**Part B – Cultural & Growth Fit (5 items)**  

| # | Check | How to verify |
|---|-------|---------------|
| 6 | Company mission aligns with your values | Read the “About” page; note 2‑3 matching points |
| 7 | Role offers learning opportunities you need | Identify at least 2 skills the job promises to develop |
| 8 | Work‑style (agile, solo, team‑lead) matches your preference | Look for keywords like “scrum”, “cross‑functional”, “ownership” |
| 9 | Growth path (promotion, mentorship) is clear | Spot language about “career ladder”, “mentor program” |
|10 | Red‑flags (high turnover, vague responsibilities) are absent | Search for “unknown”, “to be defined”, or unusually high churn news |

*Use the table to score each item (Yes = 1, No = 0). A total ≥ 7 suggests a good fit.*

## 4️⃣ Interview‑Prep – 20 Common Questions + Tips  

**Set 1**  

1. *Tell me about yourself.* – Structure: 1 min background → 2 min key achievements → 1 min relevance to role.  
2. *Why do you want to work at <<Company>>?* – Cite 2‑3 specific initiatives from the company’s “About” page.  
3. *What are your greatest strengths?* – Choose strengths that map to the top 2 job‑requirements; give a brief example.  
4. *What is your biggest weakness?* – Pick a non‑critical skill, describe improvement steps, show progress.  
5. *Describe a challenging project and how you solved it.* – Use STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result (quantify impact).

**Set 2**  

6. *How do you prioritize multiple tasks?* – Mention a simple framework (e.g., Eisenhower matrix) with a real‑world example.  
7. *Explain a technical concept (e.g., REST API) to a non‑technical person.* – Use an analogy; keep it under 30 seconds.  
8. *Give an example of teamwork where you faced conflict.* – Highlight listening, empathy, and a win‑win resolution.  
9. *What motivates you in your work?* – Align personal motivators with the role’s core activities.  
10. *Where do you see yourself in 3 years?* – Show ambition that stays within the company’s growth path.

**Set 3**  

11. *Describe a time you failed and what you learned.* – Focus on accountability and concrete lessons.  
12. *How do you stay updated with industry trends?* – List 2‑3 free resources (e.g., newsletters, GitHub repos).  
13. *What is your experience with <<Key Tool/Technology>>?* – Provide a short project snapshot, mention depth of use.  
14. *How would you handle an ambiguous requirement?* – Talk about clarifying questions and iterative prototyping.  
15. *Tell us about a time you took initiative.* – Show proactive problem‑identification and outcome.

**Set 4**  

16. *What do you know about our product/service?* – Summarize the core offering in one sentence, add a recent highlight.  
17. *How do you handle tight deadlines?* – Mention planning, breaking work into sprints, and communication.  
18. *Give an example of a data‑driven decision you made.* – Cite the metric, analysis method, and result.  
19. *What questions do you have for us?* – Prepare 2‑3 thoughtful questions about team structure, success metrics, or upcoming projects.  
20. *Why should we hire you over other candidates?* – Combine unique skill blend, cultural fit, and proven results.

*Tip:* Practice each answer aloud, keep responses under 2 minutes, and embed quantifiable results wherever possible.

## 5️⃣ 4‑Week Skill‑Gap Plan  

### Week 1 – Foundations (English)  
- **Goal:** Close basic gaps in the core technology stack.  
- **Activities (≤ 5):**  
  1. Complete the free “Intro to <<Technology>>” course on Coursera (4 hrs).  
  2. Build a mini‑project (e.g., CRUD app) using the same stack; push code to GitHub.  
  3. Write a 300‑word blog post summarizing what you learned (helps articulation).  
  4. Review the company’s tech blog for any proprietary tools; note 2‑3 keywords.  
  5. Schedule a 30‑min mock‑coding interview on Pramp.

### Week 2 – Applied Practice (English)  
- **Goal:** Translate knowledge into real‑world scenarios.  
- **Activities:**  
  1. Contribute a bug‑fix or documentation update to an open‑source project that uses the same tech.  
  2. Solve 5 problems on LeetCode tagged with the relevant language/algorithm.  
  3. Record a short video (2 min) explaining the mini‑project architecture; share with a peer for feedback.  
  4. Draft a STAR story for a past project that showcases the new skill.  
  5. Read one industry article (e.g., “State of <<Tech>> 2024”) and summarize key trends.

### Week 3 – Soft‑Skill Alignment (Telugu)  
- **Goal:** Strengthen communication and cultural fit.  
- **చర్యలు (≤ 5):**  
  1. ప్రతి రోజు 10 నిమిషాలు “Elevator Pitch”ను తెలుగులో, ఆంగ్లంలో ప్రాక్టీస్ చేయండి.  
  2. కంపెనీ విలువలపై 2‑3 వాక్యాలు తెలుగులో రాసి, వాటిని మీ రిజ్యూమే/కవర్ లెటర్‌లో ఎలా చేర్చాలో ఆలోచించండి.  
  3. ఒక మాక్ ఇంటర్వ్యూ (తెలుగులో) నిర్వహించి, ఫీడ్‌బ్యాక్ తీసుకోండి.  
  4. “STAR” పద్ధతిని తెలుగులో సంక్షిప్తంగా రాసి, 3 ఉదాహరణలను సిద్ధం చేయండి.  
  5. LinkedIn లోని కంపెనీ ఉద్యోగుల పోస్ట్‌లను చదివి, వారి కమ్యూనికేషన్ స్టైల్‌ను గమనించండి.

### Week 4 – Final Polish & Application (English)  
- **Goal:** Produce application‑ready documents and rehearse.  
- **Activities:**  
  1. Use the LaTeX CV template to create a role‑specific résumé (`cv/main_<company>_<role>.tex`).  
  2. Fill the cover‑letter template (`cover_letters/cover_<company>_<role>.tex`) with concrete examples from Weeks 1‑3.  
  3. Run a full mock interview (technical + behavioral) with a peer; record and review.  
  4. Update the 10‑point job‑fit checklist for the target posting; ensure ≥ 7 matches.  
  5. Submit the application and log the submission in the `/apply` archive as described in `07‑interview‑prep.md`.
