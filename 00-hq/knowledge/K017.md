# Kalpa Brand Voice Guide v1  
*(Telugu‑first warmth, Kalpavruksha theme)*  

> **Purpose** – This guide gives every team member, partner and tool the single, consistent voice Kalpa uses when speaking to customers, prospects and the world.  
> **Scope** – All public‑facing content: WhatsApp templates, social posts, website copy, email newsletters, chatbot replies, sales pages and workshop scripts.  
> **Audience** – Anyone who writes, speaks or designs for Kalpa.

---

## 1. Brand Voice Overview  

| Element | What it means | How it feels |
|---------|---------------|--------------|
| **Warmth** | Friendly, approachable, and genuinely helpful. | “Hey, I’m here to help you grow your salon.” |
| **Local‑centric** | Speak in Telugu first, then English if needed. | “మీ సలూన్‌కు సరైన టెక్నాలజీ ఎంచుకోవడంలో సహాయపడతాం.” |
| **Empowering** | Encourage action, show confidence in solutions. | “ఈ 14‑రోజుల ఫ్రీ పైలట్‌తో మీ వ్యాపారం ఎలా మెరుగుపడుతుందో చూడండి.” |
| **Transparent** | No hidden fees, no over‑promising. | “మొదటి 14 రోజులు ఉచితం. తర్వాత ₹1,499/నెల.” |
| **Respectful** | Use polite forms, honor local customs. | “దయచేసి మీ అభిప్రాయాన్ని పంచుకోండి.” |

## 3. Tone & Style  

| Style | Do | Don’t |
|-------|----|-------|
| **Telugu‑first** | Telugu first, English second. | English only. |
| **Clear & concise** | Short sentences, bullet points. | Long, jargon‑heavy paragraphs. |
| **No exaggeration** | Stick to verified facts. | “30% growth” or “20% increase.” |
| **No fixed daily counts** | “Posts approved by the salon.” | “3 daily posts.” |
| **Free‑policy** | Mention “free pilot” (14 days) and “free tools.” | “Free money” or “free income.” |
| **Opt‑out** | “Reply STOP to opt‑out.” | “Reply YES” only. |

## 5. Words We Never Use  

| Category | Words / Phrases | Reason |
|----------|-----------------|--------|
| **Unverified metrics** | “30% growth”, “20% increase” | Not verified in repo. |
| **Fixed daily counts** | “3 daily posts” | Repo says “posts approved by the salon.” |
| **Free‑money claims** | “free money”, “free income” | Violates free‑policy rule. |
| **Unverified claims** | “30% growth”, “20% increase” | Same as above. |
| **Any other unverified claim** | – | Must stay factual. |

## 6. Opt‑Out Mechanism (TRA‑I/DPDP compliant)

All WhatsApp and SMS templates must include:

```
మీరు భవిష్యత్తులో సందేశాలు అందుకోవద్దని కోరుకుంటే, ఈ సందేశానికి STOP అని రాయండి.
```

*Example template snippet:*

```
హలో {{name}}, మీ సలూన్‌కు కొత్త డీల్ ఉంది!  
Reply STOP to opt‑out.
```

- **STOP** is the only keyword that stops future messages.  
- No other keyword (e.g., YES, NO) is accepted for opt‑out.  
- The opt‑out must be processed within 24 hours and confirmed with a “You have been unsubscribed” message.
