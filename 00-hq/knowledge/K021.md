## 📓 Paper‑Trading Journal Template  
*(Use this sheet every time you run a paper‑trade.  Keep it in a spreadsheet or a simple note‑app.  Do **not** write any trade ideas or recommendations – just record the facts and your learning.)*

**Field notes**

- **Date** – calendar day of the paper‑trade.  
- **Symbol** – ticker or code (use a placeholder if you don’t want to reveal the actual security).  
- **Entry / Exit** – “Entry” when you would buy, “Exit” when you would sell.  
- **Qty** – number of units you would trade.  
- **Price** – the price you would pay or receive.  
- **Rationale** – a short sentence that explains the logic you used (e.g., “trend reversal signal”).  
- **Risk‑Management** – stop‑loss level, target level, or any other rule you would apply.  
- **Outcome** – the paper‑profit or loss calculated from the entry and exit prices.  
- **Lesson Learned** – what you discovered (e.g., “stop‑loss was too tight” or “signal was noisy”).

> **Tip:** Keep the journal separate from any actual trading account.  It is a learning tool only.

## 📅 Weekly Review Checklist  
*(Run this once a week to keep your paper‑trading practice disciplined and SEBI‑safe.)*

1. **Count the trades** – How many paper‑trades did you record this week?  
2. **Check rule‑adherence** – Did every trade follow your predefined entry/exit and risk rules?  
3. **Review outcomes** – What was the net paper‑profit/loss for the week?  
4. **Identify patterns** – Are there recurring reasons for losses or gains?  
5. **Plan next week** – Note one small adjustment (e.g., tighten stop‑loss, refine signal) for the following week.

> **Remember:** This checklist is for self‑education only.  It does not constitute investment advice or a trading signal.
