### Privacy Policy Summary

Tab Lifeboat stores your tab URLs, page titles, and tab group names locally inside your browser using Chrome extension storage APIs. 

* **Where your data lives:** All saved sessions, snapshots, and search indexes remain strictly on your local device.
* **Network activity:** The extension makes no external network requests and runs zero background analytics or tracking scripts.
* **Permissions used:** 
  * `tabs` and `tabGroups`: Used exclusively to read open tabs, save sessions, and restore group organization.
  * `storage`: Used to save your tab lists and extension settings locally.
  * `alarms`: Used to trigger periodic 5-minute automatic tab snapshots.
  * `downloads` (optional): Used strictly to save local backup files directly to your Downloads folder.
* **Data deletion:** Uninstalling the extension or clearing extension storage permanently removes all saved tabs and snapshot history from your machine.

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Part 1: Core Features and Privacy

1. **How does Tab Lifeboat save my open tabs?**
   Click the Tab Lifeboat icon in your browser toolbar and select **Save All**. The extension instantly records your active windows, open tabs, and tab group names and colors, allowing you to close tabs safely and free up system memory.

2. **Can I import existing data from OneTab or Session Buddy?**
   Yes. Tab Lifeboat includes built-in importers for OneTab text exports as well as Session Buddy v3 and v4 backup files. You can copy and paste your saved lists or upload the file on the options page.

3. **Does Tab Lifeboat store my browsing history on external servers?**
   No. All saved session names, URLs, page titles, and snapshot histories stay on your local device. The extension does not collect, track, or transmit your browsing data anywhere.

4. **How do tab group colors and names work when restoring?**
   When you save a browser window containing tab groups, Tab Lifeboat records the group labels and assigned colors. Restoring that session recreates the original tab groups with matching colors.

5. **Does the free version limit how many tabs or sessions I can save?**
   No. You can save unlimited tabs, windows, and custom session names manually on the free plan.

#### Part 2: Backups, Search, and Pro Features

1. **How do automatic snapshots work?**
   Tab Lifeboat takes an automatic snapshot of your active browser session every 5 minutes. Free users keep the 3 most recent snapshots, while Pro accounts store up to 90 days of snapshot history.

2. **How does tab search work across my saved sessions?**
   Open the options page and enter a keyword into the **Find a tab** search bar. Tab Lifeboat searches through every saved session, listing matching page titles and web addresses instantly.

3. **What happens during tab cleanup for duplicate or stale tabs?**
   The cleanup tool scans your active browser window for identical open tabs or tabs you have not viewed in 7 days. Before closing any tabs, Tab Lifeboat creates a session named **Before cleanup** so you can undo the action anytime.

4. **How do local file backups work?**
