### 2. The 6 Charts Explained (Plain English)

1. **Spend by Provider & Model (The Pie Chart):**  
   Shows where every rupee goes across underlying foundation models. If OpenAI GPT-4o consumes 80% of the budget while handling tasks suited for a smaller model, this chart highlights the exact margin leak.
2. **Feature Unit Economics (The Bar Graph):**  
   Maps total token spend against specific product features—such as "Auto-Draft Email" versus "Chatbot Support." It reveals which internal features subsidize others and identifies loss-making user tiers.
3. **Token Ratio Efficiency (Scatter Plot):**  
   Compares input (prompt) tokens to output (completion) tokens. A high ratio of input tokens indicates unoptimized system prompts or missing semantic caching layers, meaning money is wasted re-sending identical context blocks.
4. **Per-Customer Margin Spread (Box Plot):**  
   Plots estimated subscription revenue against per-customer LLM API consumption (using hashed customer IDs). It flags "whale" accounts whose AI query volume costs more than their monthly subscription fee.
5. **Geographic Routing & Latency Map (Bubble Chart):**  
   Displays which regions and cloud endpoints process queries. It flags data residency risks under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and identifies unexpected cross-border latency fees.
6. **Compliance Risk Index (Radar Chart):**  
   Scores exposure across four vectors: unmasked PII in logs, lack of EU AI Act Art. 50 disclosure markers, missing audit trails, and undocumented reverse-charge GST exposure.
