## 3️⃣ Daily Flow (Enquiry → Budget/Area/BHK → Site‑Visit Booking → Agent Alert)

1. **Lead enters** – via WhatsApp Cloud API, website chat widget, or Telegram bot.  
2. **Bot greets** in the prospect’s language (Telugu/Hindi/English) and asks:  
   *“What’s your budget range?”* → stores **budget**.  
   *“Which locality are you interested in?”* → stores **area**.  
   *“How many BHK do you need?”* → stores **BHK**.  
3. **Qualification** – if any answer is missing, BotBhai politely repeats until it has all three fields.  
4. **Site‑visit options** – Bot shows the next **3 available slots** (date + time) pulled from the **capacity‑lock** table.  
5. **User picks** a slot → Bot creates a **booking record** in Supabase, locks the slot, and sends a **confirmation message** (utility template).  
6. **Reminder schedule** – automated reminders at **24 hrs** and **2 hrs** before the visit (free within 24‑hour window).  
7. **Agent alert** – a push notification appears on the **owner dashboard** and an optional WhatsApp alert (template) is sent to the assigned agent.  
8. **Human takeover** – if the prospect types “talk to a human”, the bot hands over the chat to the live inbox; the agent can resume the conversation instantly.  
9. **Post‑visit follow‑up** – after the visit, BotBhai asks for feedback (optional) and logs it as a **lead status** (visited / not‑interested / hot).  

> **⚙️ All data lives in Supabase (free tier).** No external SaaS, no hidden fees.
