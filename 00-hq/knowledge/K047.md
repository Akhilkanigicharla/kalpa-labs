Every role operates using free tools only. No followers are bought, TRAI/DPDP rules are respected, and owner approval (Akhil for Kalpa, local business owner for BotBhai) is mandatory prior to publishing.

### Role 1: Strategist
*   **Weekly Responsibility:** Audits past week's growth metrics, sets weekly experimentation hypotheses, defines messaging angles for each active stream (or local client), and updates the North Star metric focus.
*   **Playbook / Skill:** Growth Hacker (Growth Strategy, Funnel Optimization, Acquisition Channels).
*   **Free Tools:** GitHub Markdown files (UNVERIFIED: free web search engines for competitor research).

### Role 2: 5-Language Copywriter
*   **Weekly Responsibility:** Drafts all social media captions, short video scripts, ad copy, and messaging loops across 5 target languages: English (EN), Telugu (TE), Hindi (HI), Tenglish (TE-EN), and Hinglish (HI-EN). Ensures mandatory ASCI `#ad` compliance for affiliate posts.
*   **Playbook / Skill:** Growth Hacker (Viral Mechanics, Copy Conversion, Multi-language Engagement).
*   **Free Tools:** Native LLM text generation capabilities (UNVERIFIED: standard free translation engines).

### Role 3: Designer Brief Writer
*   **Weekly Responsibility:** Converts text concepts into exact visual design briefs, prompts, layout specs, visual asset requirements, and reel storyboard directions for human/AI creative production.
*   **Playbook / Skill:** Growth Hacker (Conversion Rate Optimization, Social Visual Mechanics).
*   **Free Tools:** Canva (free tier) (UNVERIFIED: open-source image generation models).

### Role 4: Scheduler
*   **Weekly Responsibility:** Structures approved assets into a clean publishing queue, formats cross-platform schedules (Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube Shorts), tags correct posting windows, and routes posts to the owner for manual/one-click signoff.
*   **Playbook / Skill:** Growth Hacker (Channel Optimization, Automation Workflows).
*   **Free Tools:** Free scheduling tool (free tiers of public social management apps) (UNVERIFIED: native platform scheduling tools like Meta Business Suite).

### Role 5: Community Replier
*   **Weekly Responsibility:** Monitors inbound comments, post replies, and direct messages across all platforms. Engages users in their target language, sends opt-in WhatsApp invite templates (DPDP/TRAI compliant), and answers customer FAQs.
*   **Playbook / Skill:** Growth Hacker (Retention, Product-Led Growth, WhatsApp Opt-in Funnels).
*   **Free Tools:** Native social media inbox interfaces (UNVERIFIED: free multi-channel web chat widgets).

### Role 6: Analyst
*   **Weekly Responsibility:** Compiles impression-to-lead conversion stats, calculates estimated CAC and viral loop performance, tracks reach across all 7 streams (or local client accounts), and delivers a 5-point weekly growth breakdown.
*   **Playbook / Skill:** Growth Hacker (Analytics & Attribution, Cohort Analysis, Growth Metrics).
*   **Free Tools:** Google Sheets (free tier) (UNVERIFIED: free privacy-focused web analytics).
