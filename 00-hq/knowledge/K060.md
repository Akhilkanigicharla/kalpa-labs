Here is the sound-effect cue plan for 9:16 Kalpa reels, designed to keep audio readable and voiceover clear.

### Sound-Effect Cue Plan (12 Rows)

1. **Trigger word/moment:** Brand name or headline start  
   * **Sound type:** Soft whoosh  
   * **Length:** 150 ms  
   * **Volume:** -14 dB below voice  

2. **Trigger word/moment:** Question mark or "Why?" / "How?"  
   * **Sound type:** Subtle rise  
   * **Length:** 300 ms  
   * **Volume:** -16 dB below voice  

3. **Trigger word/moment:** Number or stat (e.g., "5", "100", "24/7")  
   * **Sound type:** Woodblock pop  
   * **Length:** 80 ms  
   * **Volume:** -12 dB below voice  

4. **Trigger word/moment:** Price or currency mention  
   * **Sound type:** Soft chime  
   * **Length:** 200 ms  
   * **Volume:** -15 dB below voice  

5. **Trigger word/moment:** "Before" (comparison contrast)  
   * **Sound type:** Low thud  
   * **Length:** 120 ms  
   * **Volume:** -18 dB below voice  

6. **Trigger word/moment:** "After" or solution revealed  
   * **Sound type:** Bright pop  
   * **Length:** 100 ms  
   * **Volume:** -12 dB below voice  

7. **Trigger word/moment:** Feature highlight or screen tap on reel  
   * **Sound type:** UI click  
   * **Length:** 50 ms  
   * **Volume:** -14 dB below voice  

8. **Trigger word/moment:** Mistake, problem, or "Don't"  
   * **Sound type:** Quick dull buzz  
   * **Length:** 150 ms  
   * **Volume:** -16 dB below voice  

9. **Trigger word/moment:** Language switcher mention (Telugu, English, Hindi...)  
   * **Sound type:** Card flip swipe  
   * **Length:** 100 ms  
   * **Volume:** -14 dB below voice  

10. **Trigger word/moment:** Fast result or speed claim  
    * **Sound type:** Fast air swish  
    * **Length:** 120 ms  
    * **Volume:** -15 dB below voice  

11. **Trigger word/moment:** Call to action / "Comment" or "Message"  
    * **Sound type:** Notification ding  
    * **Length:** 250 ms  
    * **Volume:** -12 dB below voice  

12. **Trigger word/moment:** Final frame freeze / handle display  
    * **Sound type:** Gentle warmth chime  
    * **Length:** 350 ms  
    * **Volume:** -14 dB below voice
