3. Long Description
Never lose your browser tabs again. Tab Lifeboat automatically creates local backups of your open windows and tab sessions so your work is safe if your browser closes or crashes. Save entire sessions, organize open tabs, and restore them whenever you need.

Move your tab history over easily by importing files from Session Buddy and OneTab. Keep your data safe on your computer with automatic local snapshots and manual JSON export files. Core session saving, organizing, and restoring features are completely free with unlimited local saving. Optional cloud backup and multi-device sync are available on a paid plan.

4. Feature Bullets
1. Automatic local backups save tab sessions quietly on your computer without storage limits.
2. One-click tab import lets you bring in export files from Session Buddy and OneTab.
3. Easy export option creates downloadable backup files of your saved tab sessions.
4. Fast tab restoration brings back single tabs, complete windows, or saved groups instantly.
5. Optional cloud backup and sync keeps your saved sessions accessible across all your devices.

5. Privacy Paragraph
Your privacy comes first. All saved tabs, windows, and session histories stay stored locally on your device by default. Tab Lifeboat does not collect, track, or share your browsing history. Your tab data only leaves your device if you explicitly enable optional paid cloud backup and sync.

7. FAQ
1. Will Tab Lifeboat slow down my browser?
No. Tab Lifeboat runs quietly in the background and only updates local storage when tabs change.

2. Is there a limit on how many tabs I can save locally?
No. You get unlimited local saving and restoration on the free version.

3. Can I move my data from another tab extension?
Yes. You can import saved tab export files directly from Session Buddy and OneTab.

4. Where is my tab data stored?
Your tab data stays strictly on your computer unless you turn on optional cloud backup and sync.
