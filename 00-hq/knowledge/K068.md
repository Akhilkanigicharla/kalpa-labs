# Sound-Effect Cue Plan for Kalpa Reels

This cue plan establishes standard rules for sound effects in 9:16 vertical explainer reels. All sound effects must be generated programmatically or sourced from CC0 public domain assets, mixed at least 12 dB below the primary voice track.

---

## Visual and Text Transitions

1. **Number or Stat Appearance**
   - **Trigger:** A single digit, percentage, or key statistic pops up on screen.
   - **Cue Type:** Soft pop.
   - **Length:** 100 ms.
   - **Volume:** -14 dB relative to voice.

2. **List Item Entry**
   - **Trigger:** A new bullet point or list card slides into view.
   - **Cue Type:** Fast whoosh.
   - **Length:** 150 ms.
   - **Volume:** -16 dB relative to voice.

3. **Scene Change**
   - **Trigger:** Full background scene shift or layout transition.
   - **Cue Type:** Deep whoosh.
   - **Length:** 250 ms.
   - **Volume:** -12 dB relative to voice.

4. **Price Display**
   - **Trigger:** A price tag or currency value appears on screen.
   - **Cue Type:** Soft chime.
   - **Length:** 200 ms.
   - **Volume:** -15 dB relative to voice.

---

## Key Moments and Highlights

5. **Warning or Mistake**
   - **Trigger:** An error icon, caution badge, or warning text shows.
   - **Cue Type:** Low thud.
   - **Length:** 180 ms.
   - **Volume:** -12 dB relative to voice.

6. **Core Solution Reveal**
   - **Trigger:** Main conclusion or primary feature text reveals.
   - **Cue Type:** Bright shimmer.
   - **Length:** 300 ms.
   - **Volume:** -14 dB relative to voice.

7. **Checkmark Completion**
   - **Trigger:** A tick mark or green check animation finishes.
   - **Cue Type:** Crisp click.
   - **Length:** 80 ms.
   - **Volume:** -16 dB relative to voice.

8. **Text Highlight Sweep**
   - **Trigger:** A visual accent line sweeps under important caption text.
   - **Cue Type:** Soft brush.
   - **Length:** 120 ms.
   - **Volume:** -18 dB relative to voice.

---

## Action Prompts and Code

9. **UI Tap or Button Press**
   - **Trigger:** On-screen cursor clicks or finger tap graphic hits a button.
   - **Cue Type:** Digital tap.
   - **Length:** 60 ms.
   - **Volume:** -15 dB relative to voice.

10. **Card Swipe**
    - **Trigger:** An image card or UI panel gets swiped away.
    - **Cue Type:** Light air swoosh.
    - **Length:** 140 ms.
    - **Volume:** -16 dB relative to voice.

11. **Call to Action**
    - **Trigger:** Final CTA frame or link prompt shows on screen.
    - **Cue Type:** Warm bell tone.
    - **Length:** 350 ms.
    - **Volume:** -12 dB relative to voice.

12. **Terminal Text Typing**
    - **Trigger:** Monospace code or terminal line types out on screen.
    - **Cue Type:** Soft mechanical tick.
    - **Length:** 40 ms per character step.
    - **Volume:** -20 dB relative to voice.

---

## Rules When NOT to Add Sound Effects

1. **During Active Speech Peaks**
   - Never trigger mid-frequency or high-frequency transient sounds directly over an accented spoken word or syllable.

2. **Rapid Sequential Elements**
   - Do not trigger sound effects for list items or text lines appearing closer than 300 ms apart to prevent clutter.

3. **Legal and Consent Disclaimers**
   - Omit sound effects completely during DPDP consent notices, compliance disclaimers, or formal policy text overlays.
