## Minority report (kept on purpose)

- **Strategy** (no): Running the pilot before K025 and K002 pass risks DPDP/TRAI breaches, WhatsApp bans, and wasted effort given Akhil's 1‑hour daily limit and no paying customers.
- **Operations** (no): A live WhatsApp pilot needs real‑time monitoring, consent handling, and failure recovery. Without Akhil's 1‑hour daily window there is no automation or support, so compliance (TRAI/DPDP) and user‑experience will break instantly.
- **Cash & risk** (no): Zero cost but missing DPDP consent and TRAI opt‑in could trigger fines, WhatsApp bans, and brand damage. Worst case: regulatory penalty and loss of channel. Waiting for K025/K002 review eliminates that risk.

## Cheapest tests and kill criteria, by seat

- **Strategy**: test: Use a personal WhatsApp number to send the opt‑in script to a friend and verify consent flow for free this week. · stop if: K025 or K002 fail review or reveal compliance gaps.
