# Brief shared by the 8 Kalpa Labs planning agents (P0–P7)

Read first: `00-hq/research/BRIEF.md` (company, constraints, licence rule), the README of your world's folder, and the research reports in `00-hq/research/github/` that touch your world. BotBhai detail lives in `01-studio/botbhai/` (docs/requirements/salon.md, setups/, 00-hq/agents/registry.js for the 215-agent org and state.json).

**Goal:** turn Kalpa Labs into a real money machine — first rupee as early as possible, then predictable monthly revenue — with the CEO's 1 hour/day, ₹0–5,000 total budget, free tiers only until going public, a low-RAM PC (all compute in the cloud), one GitHub account (Akhilkanigicharla). No paid sign-ups or domains until revenue justifies them; say when a spend is worth it and what it unlocks.

**Hard rules for every plan:** legal and policy-compliant in India (DPDP Act 2023, TRAI, SEBI, GST, Meta/WhatsApp Business policy, Chrome Web Store, Play Store); no scraping of Google Maps/Justdial/Instagram or consumer personal data; no guaranteed-returns claims; licences per BRIEF.md. Check anything that describes the world as it is now (prices, policies, competitor pricing, free-tier limits) with WebSearch before stating it, and cite sources at the end.

**Every plan file must contain, in this order:**
1. **One-paragraph verdict** — how this world makes money, realistic ₹ in month 1 / 3 / 6 / 12 (low / likely / high) and the single biggest risk.
2. **Customer & offer** — who pays, the exact offer, price ladder (₹), what they get, why they buy now; 3 named competitor alternatives with their prices.
3. **Unit economics** — cost to serve per customer per month on free tiers and after limits; gross margin; break-even customer count; where the free tiers run out.
4. **Go-to-market** — the first 10 customers step by step (channels, scripts outline, daily quota fitting in ~20 min/day), then 10→100.
5. **Build plan** — milestones as quests sized for ≤ 60 min of CEO time each (id, title, CEO action vs agent action, done-check), grouped Week 1, Weeks 2–4, Month 2, Month 3. Reference concrete repo paths and the research picks (ADOPT items).
6. **Agent swarm** — the agents (role, runs_as code/claude/human, cadence, validator) this world needs, in the style of `00-hq/agents/registry.js`.
7. **Metrics & kill criteria** — weekly numbers to watch, and the dated condition under which this world is paused so time moves to a better one.
8. **What I need from Akhil** — a short checklist (accounts, approvals, decisions), never passwords/OTPs/keys in chat.
9. **Sources**.

Write plain, specific English (the CEO reads on a phone). Tables where comparing. 1,500–3,000 words. Do not edit any file other than your own plan file. Never sign up for anything or use credentials.
