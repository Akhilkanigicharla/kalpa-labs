# Competitors per world, and how to tweak each to win (11 Nov 2026)

Akhil asked: "evaluate our ideas with competitors and tweak every world to beat competitors." Method: web search today, 5 worlds, public pages only (no banned scraping). Prices are from comparison and review sites unless marked "own page"; re-check a vendor's own page before quoting any of them to a customer. Tweaks are PROPOSALS until Akhil says yes (rule 0).

## b1 BotBhai: the real competitor is salon/gym software, not chatbot platforms
| Competitor | What they sell | Price per month | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| SalonBoost | appointments, WhatsApp reminders + bills, POS, staff | ₹499 to ₹999 (Software Advice) or ₹799 to ₹1,199 (Capterra): sources disagree | softwareadvice.com, capterra.com |
| Salonist | booking + POS + CRM | ₹1,056 to ₹1,887 | codingclave.com guide 2026 |
| MioSalon | CRM + marketing automation | ₹999 to ₹2,500 | codingclave.com |
| Dingg | GST billing, WhatsApp API, UPI | ₹2,000 to ₹4,000 flat | dingg.app (own blog) |
| Gym software (market range) | members, reminders, billing | ₹1,000 to ₹25,000+ | easygymsoftware.com 2026 |
| AiSensy / Wati / Interakt | WhatsApp API + chatbot builder | from ₹1,500 / ₹1,999 / ₹2,499 | richautomate.in Nov 2026 |
**What this means:** at ₹1,499 a month BotBhai costs as much as a full salon suite that already sends WhatsApp reminders. On features per rupee we lose.
**Where they are weak:** all are self-serve software the owner must learn; several charge extra per staff login (₹200 to ₹500, codingclave.com); none found that advertises replies in Telugu script + Tenglish.
**Tweaks:** (1) Position as the front desk that answers and books, working next to whatever billing software they have, not as management software. (2) Done-for-you: we set it up and run it; owner learns nothing. (3) 5-language replies as the headline. (4) Entry price must sit clearly under the suites: the ₹999 Lite monthly or the one-time starter pack, with ₹1,499 as the upgrade. (5) No per-staff fee, said out loud.

| GetVidya | AI-adaptive practice, 240+ mocks | ₹149 a month | getvidya.in (own page) |
**What this means:** we can never match their test counts.
**Where they are weak:** all are subscriptions that end (Testbook: access stops when the pass expires); all sell tests, not a plan.
**Tweaks:** (1) Sell what they do not: a one-time ₹199 to ₹299 planner + tracker + exam-date alerts, yours forever. (2) Stay narrow: AP and Telangana state exams, in Telugu. (3) Be a companion, not a rival: the kit points to free mock tests elsewhere. (4) Say "one payment, no expiry" on the page.

## b4 Tab Lifeboat: the leaders are free
| Competitor | Users | Price | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| OneTab | 2 million | free | Chrome Web Store numbers via rabbitpair.com |
| Session Buddy | 1 million | free | same |
| Tab Session Manager | not found | free, open source | supasidebar.com |
| Workona | 500,000 | paid, $8 to $12 a month | chrome-tips.pages.dev, nexasphere.io |
**What this means:** charging for plain session saving will not work; three free tools do it.
**Opening:** Session Buddy's v4 rewrite lost users' saved sessions and people are looking for alternatives (northlabapps.com, July 2026; one source, vendor blog).
**Tweaks:** (1) Lead with "never lose your tabs" and prove it: automatic local backups + export file. (2) One-click import from Session Buddy and OneTab. (3) Keep saving and restoring free without the 5-a-day limit; charge only for cloud backup and sync across devices. (4) Lifetime licence option.

**What this means:** big channels post faster and more than one person ever can; Telugu-language deal channels already exist.
**Where they are weak:** volume channels flood the feed; none found that explains whether a deal is actually good.
**Tweaks:** (1) Few deals a day, each checked: price history, true EMI cost, card-offer maths from our calculators. "Is this deal real?" in Telugu. (2) Use the calculators as the hook, deals as the follow-up. (3) Weight towards categories that pay more (fashion, books, home). EarnKaro headlines of 10 to 15% are "up to" figures: unverified.

## b6 AI cost + guardrails consulting: the tools are free, so sell the doing
| Competitor | Free tier | Paid from | Note | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Langfuse | 50,000 units a month; self-host free (MIT) | $29 a month | bought by ClickHouse, Jan 2026 | promptquorum.com, costbench.com |
| Helicone | 10,000 requests a month; self-host free (Apache 2.0) | $79 a month | bought by Mintlify, 2026 | costbench.com Oct 2026, usagepricing.com |
| Portkey | 10,000 requests a month | $49 a month | bought by Palo Alto Networks, 2026 | buildmvpfast.com, usagepricing.com |
**What this means:** a cost dashboard of our own has no market; excellent ones are free and open source.
**Where they are weak:** they are tools. Someone still has to install them, read the numbers, change the prompts and models, and write the DPDP paperwork.
**Tweaks:** (1) Drop "our tool" from the pitch; install Langfuse or Helicone for the client (both licences allow it) and deliver the savings plan. (2) Pair it with the India-specific part no US tool gives: DPDP notice, AI usage policy, model register. (3) Savings-share fee for the first clients.
