## What the numbers say overall
The ways with the shortest path to a first rupee are the one-time, low-price, face-to-face ones (b1 rows 1 and 2), because they need no audience. Everything that depends on traffic (b5, b3 online, b4) needs hundreds to thousands of visitors first.
