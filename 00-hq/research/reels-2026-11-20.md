## Momentum 30/20 result (report `07-trading/reports/momentum-30-20-2026-11-20.md`)

Setup: today's Nifty 50, ₹1,00,000, all NSE charges, monthly rebalance.

| Period | Momentum 30/20 | With Nifty 200-day filter | Buy and hold NIFTYBEES |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2011–2026 | 20.1%/yr, worst fall −36% | 8.5%/yr, worst fall −12% | 11.1%/yr, worst fall −36% |
| 2019–2026 | 18.4%/yr | 10.4%/yr | 12.8%/yr |

It beat the Nifty by about 9 points a year over 2011–26 and about 5.6 points over 2019–26. The reel's "10%" is about right, but three things flatter it:
1. **Survivorship bias.** The list is today's Nifty 50, which includes later winners such as Trent, BEL and Shriram Finance.
2. **Tax.** About 200 trades a year means most gains are short-term and taxed at 20% (not counted).
3. **Capital.** It needs about ₹1 lakh to hold 20 stocks in whole shares.

**The cheaper route for ₹5–10k is a Nifty momentum index ETF** (verify which ones exist, their costs and liquidity first; A297).
