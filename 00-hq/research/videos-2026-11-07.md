### Update 14:45: Akhil's screenshots name the repos
**1. nateherkai/roast-room** (shown in the 100x short). Checked 8 Nov: MIT licence file, commit e40f406, README and skills read.
- How it works: you are the Chair. 7 default seats (+5 on call) each own one question: strategy/reversibility, do people want it, will they pay, where attention comes from, who does it and what it costs your time, can it run without you, cash and risk. Three rounds: open, debate, vote (ratify a draft resolution). The Chair scores options, keeps the dissent ("minority report"), adds next steps, the cheapest test and kill criteria. A unanimous first round is treated as a warning, so the strongest unargued option gets argued on purpose.
- Limits for us: it runs on Claude Code (Claude tokens, against rule 11), and each seat is built from a real person's public work, with a cartoon portrait made from their photo. We won't copy that part.
- **Use (A182):** take the method and the seven questions (MIT, credit given), run the seats on 5 different free models, name the seats by role (Strategy, Customer, Pricing, Marketing, Time, Operations, Cash), not by person.

**2. JustVugg/colibri** (heyikjot reel, "744B on a laptop"). Checked 8 Nov: Apache-2.0 licence, README read.
- Pure-C engine that keeps the active part of a huge mixture-of-experts model in RAM and reads the rest from disk.
- Its own table: GLM-5.2 (744B) is a **429 GB download** and runs at **0.05-0.1 tokens/s cold on a 25 GB laptop** (one paragraph would take over an hour), 1.83 tok/s on a 128 GB machine. Smallest useful chat model: Qwen3.6-35B, 23 GB download, 10-20 GB RAM, 6 tok/s on CPU.
- **Verdict: not for us now.** Akhil's PC is low-spec and nothing gets installed on it (rule 2); GitHub runners have about 14 GB disk. Free OpenRouter/Groq models are far faster. Keep it in mind only if we ever need a fully offline/private model on a big server.
