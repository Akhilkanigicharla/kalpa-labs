Questions

Straight answers

Will my customers know it's a bot?

Yes. BotBhai says it is an automated assistant at the start of a chat, and hands over to your staff whenever asked.

What if it gives a wrong answer?

It only uses the prices and timings you give us. If it is not sure, or the customer is upset, it hands the chat to you instead of guessing.

Do I need a computer or a new phone number?

No computer. You need one WhatsApp number for the salon; we help you set it up during the pilot.

Is my customers' data safe?

We follow India's DPDP Act: we act only on your instructions, keep data in your salon's account, and delete it when you ask. Messages go only to customers who opted in.

You are new. Why should I trust you?

Fair question. We have no customers yet, so the first salons get a free pilot and pay nothing unless it helps. You can stop any time.
