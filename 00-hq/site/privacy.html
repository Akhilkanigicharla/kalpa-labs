Who we are

Kalpa Labs, a sole proprietorship of Akhil Kanigicharla, Hyderabad, Telangana, India. Akhil answers every data question and complaint: aakhiil51458@gmail.com.

What we collect and why

  • Contact form: what you need, your name and your WhatsApp number, and your tick on the consent box. Why: to reply to your request on WhatsApp or by phone.
  • Website chat: the messages you type are sent to an AI model provider only so it can write the reply. We do not save the chat text. Please don't type passwords, card numbers or health details.
  • Callback in chat: your name, phone number, what you need and the best time to call, saved only after you say yes to being contacted.
  • Error reports: if the page breaks, your browser sends us the error message, file and line number. No name, number or chat text.
  • Technical logs: our host records the IP address, time and page of each visit for security and to stop abuse.

We don't sell your data, we don't show ads and we don't use tracking or analytics tools.

Who helps us

  • Netlify (USA): hosts the site, stores form and callback details, keeps technical logs.
  • Groq (USA), with OpenRouter (USA) as a backup: write the chat replies. They receive the chat text, under their own terms.

Under 18

This site and our chat are for people aged 18 or older. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us. If you tell the chat you are under 18, it stops, asks for a parent or guardian, and saves nothing.

How long we keep it

Form and callback details are deleted 12 months after our last contact with you, unless you become a customer. Customer details are kept as your service agreement says.

Delete or stop, with one message

Email aakhiil51458@gmail.com or reply DELETE on WhatsApp to any message from us. We stop contacting you, delete your details and tell you when it is done. Saying no never stops you from using the site or the chat.

Your rights

You can ask to see a summary of your data and who it was shared with, correct it, delete it, or name someone to act for you if you die or cannot act. Write to the email above. We reply within 48 hours and finish within 30 days, never later than 90 days.

Complaints

If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Changes

We post changes here with a new date. Big changes are shown on the site before they apply.
