Voice agent

        
On the client's live site the voice agent listens through the browser microphone (Chrome / Android, English-India) and speaks replies. WhatsApp voice notes are transcribed for free with Groq Whisper, so customers can just talk. Phone-call agents need a paid telephony number (Exotel / Plivo) and are a paid add-on for clients later.

Pulls salons, gyms, estate agents and coaching centres for a city from OpenStreetMap (free and legal to reuse, with credit), scores each one, and writes an Excel sheet with filters, colour-coded scores and a status dropdown.
