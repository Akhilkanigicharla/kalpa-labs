BotBhai walk-in · Cluster 1 · SR Nagar / Ameerpet

3 mapped salons + 5 you spot, about 1.1 km on foot

Start: Ameerpet Metro station · Best time 11:30 AM–1 PM, when salons are quiet · Date: __________

At each salon (10–12 min)
  1. Ask for the owner. If busy, leave the card and ask when to come back.
  2. Ask: How many WhatsApps come in after 9 PM? How many no-shows last Saturday? Who answers the phone during a facial?
  3. Demo on their phone: type "repu haircut kavali" and book.
  4. Leave the A5 card; write their 3 answers on it.
Rules
  • Walk-in only: no cold calls, no WhatsApp to numbers they didn't give you.
  • Never promise a number of bookings; prices only from the card.
  • At most 3 follow-ups (days 2, 5, 10); stop on "no".
  • Two blocks of 4 if time is short. Reserve if one is closed: none.
