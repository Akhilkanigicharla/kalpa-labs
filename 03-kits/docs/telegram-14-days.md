# Telegram channel: first 14 days of daily questions (A22 · K-04)

**Channel:** "Library of Ranks" (created by Akhil in C09). One original GATE-style CS question a day at 7:30 AM IST, posted as a Telegram **quiz poll** with the correct option set. The next day's post opens with yesterday's answer and a one-line explanation.

**Source of questions:** `telegram-14-days.csv`. All 14 are written by us, not copied from past GATE papers. Every answer is worked out in the explanation column, and `03-kits/docs/test_questions.py` re-checks the ones that can be computed.

**Post template (the A18 poster fills `{days}` and the link):**

```
📚 Day {day} · {subject} · {days} days to GATE 2027
{question}
(quiz poll: A / B / C / D)

Yesterday's answer: {prev_answer}: {prev_explanation}
Free GATE 2027 tracker (Lite): {lite_link}
```

**Rules:**
- No "only today", countdown-sale or rank-guarantee wording (Dark Patterns Guidelines 2023).
- The kit link appears only in the footer.
- At most one post a day.
- Telugu version later, after the C17 read-through.
