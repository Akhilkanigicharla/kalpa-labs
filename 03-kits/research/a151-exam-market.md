Research run: 6 Nov 2026 (cloud session). Status: **draft. Akhil picks the first exam.**
Rule: two sources per number; *(1 source)* = not yet double-checked; "not checked" = no number gathered this run.
Kit (Akhil's words, VISION.md) = study tracker + reminders + status/progress + exam alerts (notifications, dates) + best material + best YouTube videos, at an affordable price.

## 1. The answer in 5 lines
1. Demand is huge: RRB NTPC 1.22 crore applicants, RRB Group D 1.08 crore, SSC CGL 28 lakh, NEET 22.8 lakh, IBPS PO 12.4 lakh, UPSC 8.2 lakh. TGPSC and APPSC each draw 4–5.6 lakh per Group exam, in Telugu.
2. Big players already sell **mock tests very cheaply**: the Testbook Pass is ₹749/year for 1,000+ exams. Kalpa must **not** compete on mock tests or video lectures.
3. The gap is the **personal side**: a day-by-day plan and tracker, WhatsApp/Telegram reminders, exact dates and alerts, and a short "best free YouTube + material" list, in **Telugu** for state exams. Big apps are built around content; nobody owns the plan-and-reminder habit in Telugu.
4. Best first-kit candidates: **(A) TGPSC Group 2/3 (Telugu)**, (B) SSC CGL, (C) RRB NTPC/Group D, (D) GATE (already built). Recommendation: **A**, because the home-state, home-language edge is real and TGPSC is expected to issue ~15 notifications.
5. Price: **₹199 per kit / ₹99 for a 3-month plan**, below Testbook's yearly pass, because the kit *adds to* the apps people already use.

## 3. What candidates pay today, and to whom
- **Testbook Pass:** ₹749 for 365 days (list price ₹1,199): 1.5 lakh+ mock tests, 30k+ old papers, 1,000+ exams. Official page fetched today; coupon sites show sale prices of ₹299–367 (zoutons).
- **Testbook exam courses:** e.g. SSC CGL 12 months at ₹1,299 sale price (list ₹4,999) *(1 source: zoutons)*.
- **Adda247 Mahapacks:** SSC CGL Mahapack ₹1,599 (list ₹7,614); SSC CGL live batch ₹1,256 (adda247 course pages, fetched today).
- **Low-cost AI rival:** GetVidya, ₹149/month with a weekly AI study plan *(1 source, its own comparison page)*. This proves people will pay for *plans*, not just tests.
- **Free:** YouTube channels, Telegram PDF groups, "Sarkari result" style alert sites. That makes **alerts alone hard to charge for**: bundle them into the kit.
- **Offline coaching:** UPSC and Group 1 coaching in Hyderabad costs much more than any app *(not checked this run)*.

## 4. Gaps Kalpa can fill (what the kit does that ₹749 apps don't)
1. **A plan, not a library:** "today: 2 topics, 1 mock, 30 min revision", built from the exam date backwards, and re-planned when you miss a day.
2. **Reminders where people already are:** WhatsApp (opt-in, templates) and Telegram, in 5 languages (Kalpa rule): Telugu, Hindi, English, Tenglish, Hinglish.
3. **One source of truth for dates:** notification, apply-by, fee, admit card, exam, answer key, result. Every date links to the official site.
4. **Curated "best of free":** per syllabus topic, 1–2 best YouTube videos + 1 free PDF/NCERT/official source, so students stop drowning in Telegram groups.
5. **Progress you can see:** syllabus % done, mock score trend, streak. The proven Google Sheet tracker from the GATE kit becomes the base.
Licensing note: link to videos and official PDFs only; never re-host coaching material (copyright).
