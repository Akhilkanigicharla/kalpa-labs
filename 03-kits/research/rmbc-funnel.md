# RMBC funnel for b3 kits (from "How I Made $295,000 With One Digital Product")

Checked 18 Oct 2026 (IST). Video: Cayãn Vanderlinde, youtube.com/watch?v=h15j9x6t0fU (uploaded 17 Oct 2026, 3:48, transcript read in full). The $295,000 is the creator's own claim; it is not verified and must never appear in our copy.

## The method in one line
Ad → video sales letter (VSL) or sales page → checkout with an order bump → upsell. Most effort goes into the VSL, written with Stefan Georgi's **RMBC**: Research, Mechanism, Brief, Copy.

## The 5 steps, mapped to Kalpa agents

| Step | What the video says | Who does it at Kalpa | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Find a problem people already pay to solve | Long-running ads (Meta Ad Library), rival products, forum complaints → "what are they frustrated with, what have they tried, what do they wish was explained" | Echo + Scout (live web search since 18 Oct) → Kavi | Public pages only; summarise, never copy review text |
| 2. Mechanism | Why buyers are still stuck, and why this product works when others didn't; pick the most believable and new | Kavi skill `rmbc-product-funnel` | Must be true of the product as built |
| 3. Brief → Copy | One-page brief (buyer, problem, mechanism, product, proof, offer); then write the script one section at a time and rewrite it | Kavi | Proof = only real proof; else "[no proof yet]" |
| 4. Ads | ~20 hook variations from the VSL, each a different pain/desire; simple creatives (text over video, clip + voice-over) | Adi skill `vsl-ad-variations`, Lens/Reel/Vani | ASCI disclosure; no income/results claims |
| 5. Checkout + numbers | Order bump that solves the next problem; upsell after purchase. Watch cost per click, watch-through to the offer, cost per purchase | Kavi (offer), Dash (numbers) | Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules: clear price, no fake scarcity or countdowns |

## Where it fits our kits
- **Tax + SIP planner** and **GATE 2027 kit** already exist (`03-kits/products/`). The method's first job is step 1: confirm the buyers' words for "what they already tried" before writing any sales copy.
