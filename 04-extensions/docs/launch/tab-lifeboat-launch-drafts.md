## 1. AlternativeTo (fields to paste)

- **Name:** Tab Lifeboat
- **Platforms:** Google Chrome (browser extension)
- **Licence:** Free · Proprietary
- **Short description:** Save every tab and tab group in one click, with automatic snapshots kept on your own computer.
- **Long description:** Tab Lifeboat saves every window, pinned tab and tab group in one click without closing anything. It takes a snapshot every few minutes, so tabs survive a crash or an update. Restore brings back group names and colours. You can move in from OneTab or Session Buddy with a preview first. Everything stays in your browser: no account, no servers, no analytics.
- **Tags:** tab-manager, session-manager, backup, privacy, productivity
- **Suggest as alternative to:** OneTab, Session Buddy (it imports from both).
