## Rule that wins users (evidence: OneTab 2M and Session Buddy 1M are fully free; Toby's paywall drew 1–2★ reviews; Partizion locked users out)
Saving, restoring, import and export stay free and unlimited forever. Saved data stays usable if Pro lapses.

## Price (data-based starting point; test after launch)
$2.99/month (₹149) · $19/year (₹999) · $24 lifetime (₹1,499).
Below Toby $6/mo, TabExtend $8/mo, Workona from $7/mo; near Raindrop $28/yr; lifetime between Tabs Outliner $15 and SupaSidebar $39.50.
INR prices are an unbenchmarked guess. Conversion: plan on ~1% (0.8% and ~3% are two single-developer reports, unverified as typical).
At 300 installs that is ~3 buyers, so Pro is for later: grow installs first with the free v0.3.
