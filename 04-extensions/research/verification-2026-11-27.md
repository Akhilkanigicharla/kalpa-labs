# Tab Lifeboat: fact check of the 27 Nov research notes

Checked 2026-11-27 by an independent session with no prior context. Each claim was checked against the provider's own page, fetched today with WebFetch, or with Firecrawl when a page was rendered by JavaScript. A second, independent source was checked as well. No logins were used. The notes checked are `pain-mining-2026-11-27.md`, `willingness-to-pay-2026-11-27.md` and `feature-ideas-2026-11-27.md`. Nothing in them was changed.

## What this changes for the pricing and feature decision
- **Edge Collections (feature-ideas F3):** the importer is still useful, because users were pushed to a CSV export. The wording needs to change from "retiring" to "removed in Edge 149 (June 2026)".
- **Workona:** Pro starts at $7/month on its own page, so the "subscriptions of $3 to $12" range still holds. The Team figure in the WTP table ($12) does not match the pricing page ($8 starting).
- **Conversion:** keep the 1% planning number, but label it an assumption built from 2 anecdotes, not a benchmark.
