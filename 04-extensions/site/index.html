Questions

OneTab lost my tabs. Can Tab Lifeboat get them back?

No tool can bring back tabs another extension has already deleted. If OneTab still shows some of them, use its "Export URLs" and paste the text into Tab Lifeboat: you get a preview, then they are saved and snapshotted from then on.

Do you see my tabs?

No. Your tab titles and links are stored in your browser on your computer and are never sent to us; we have no servers. Details are in the privacy policy.

What happens if I uninstall?

Chrome deletes the extension's data on uninstall, ours included. Download the backup file first (Backups and settings → Backup file) and restore it after you reinstall.
