Privacy policy

Last updated {{UPDATED}}. Applies to the Tab Lifeboat Chrome extension and this website.

In short

Your tabs stay on your computer. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads, and we never sell or share your data.

What the extension handles, and why

DataWhyWhere it is kept
Titles and links (URLs) of your open tabs, tab group names and colours, window layoutOnly to save and restore your sessions and snapshots, which is the extension's single purposeIn your browser on your computer (IndexedDB). Never sent to us.
Files you choose to import (OneTab text, Session Buddy file) or a Tab Lifeboat backup fileTo add those sessionsRead on your computer; the sessions are stored as above
Settings and snapshot timesTo run automatic snapshotsIn your browser on your computer

Permissions it asks Chrome for: tabs and tabGroups (read and reopen your tabs and groups), alarms (timed snapshots), storage and unlimitedStorage (keep large sessions safely). The free extension makes no network requests.

When Pro opens

  • Licence check: your licence key and a device label are sent to Dodo Payments to confirm the key. Your purchase details (name, email, payment) are collected and processed by Dodo Payments as merchant of record under its own privacy policy; we receive the order record.
  • Google Drive backup (optional): if you switch it on, a backup file is saved to your Google Drive using the drive.file permission, which only reaches files Tab Lifeboat created. We cannot see the rest of your Drive, and the file never passes through us.

Limited Use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Tab Lifeboat's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

We do not sell user data, do not use or transfer it for advertising, credit checks or any purpose unrelated to saving and restoring your tabs, and no person reads it.

Deleting your data

Delete any session inside the extension. Uninstalling Tab Lifeboat deletes all of its data from your browser. Backup files you downloaded or saved to Google Drive are yours: delete them like any other file.

Contact and grievances
