Moving from OneTab

Bring every saved OneTab list into Tab Lifeboat in about two minutes. Nothing is uploaded: the move happens on your computer.

1. Export from OneTab

  1. Open your OneTab page and click Export / Import URLs (top right).
  2. Select all the text in the Export URLs box and copy it.

2. Import into Tab Lifeboat

  1. Install Tab Lifeboat from the Chrome Web Store (free).
  2. Right-click the Tab Lifeboat icon and choose Options, then find Move in from OneTab or Session Buddy.
  3. Paste the text into the box, or choose a saved .txt file. Tab Lifeboat shows how many tabs and groups it found before you import.
  4. Click Import. Each OneTab group becomes its own saved group, with titles kept.

Good to know

  • Lines that are not web links are skipped and counted, so you can see if anything was left out.
  • Keep your OneTab list until you have checked the import; you can delete it afterwards.
  • From now on Tab Lifeboat takes automatic snapshots, and you can download a backup file any time.
