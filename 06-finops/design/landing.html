Questions

Do you need our prompts?

No. Cost needs only model and token counts. Please drop prompt and reply columns before you send; our importer reads only the usage fields and ignores the rest.

Why rupees, not dollars?

Your customers pay you in rupees. We convert each day's spend at that day's RBI/FBIL reference rate, so margin per customer is real.

What is the DPDP score?

20 yes/no questions about how your AI features handle personal data under the DPDP Act 2023 and Rules. It is a readiness checklist, not legal advice.

We already use Langfuse or Helicone.

Keep it. We read its export and add rupees, margins and the evidence pack on top.
