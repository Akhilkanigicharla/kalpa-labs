## 4. A327: the full best mix inside the EA (21 Nov 2026)

New file: `Execution/CMixEngine.mqh`. It runs the 12 other strategies of the A323 mix next to the EA's own EA+MSNR H1 strategy, each with its own magic number (MagicNumber + 100 + i).

| # | Strategy | Chart | Order | Waits | Max hold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | MSNR fresh level, buy | H1 | buy limit at the level | 20 bars | 48 bars |
| 1 / 2 | MSNR QM, buy / sell | H1 | limit at the V level | 20 | 48 |
| 3 | 9 EMA + MSNR rejection, both sides | H2 | market, next bar | – | 24 |
| 4 / 5 | MSNR fresh level, buy / sell | H2 | limit | 10 | 24 |
| 6 | MSNR QM, buy | H2 | limit | 10 | 24 |
| 7 | 9 EMA + MSNR rejection, both sides | H4 | market | – | 12 |
| 8 / 9 | MSNR fresh level, buy / sell | H4 | limit | 10 | 20 |
| 10 / 11 | MSNR QM, buy / sell | H4 | limit | 10 | 20 |

**Rules for every mix trade**
- Stops, targets (2R) and the 9 EMA filter are exactly as in the research.
- Risk is 0.25% of equity per trade (`inp_MixRiskPct`), 1.5× after a win and 0.75× after a loss.
- Total open risk is capped at 3% (`inp_MixMaxOpenRiskPct`).
- If the minimum lot would risk more than 1.5× the target, the trade is skipped.
- No new orders during the ±2 h news blackout; pending orders are cancelled when a blackout starts.
- No new orders while the EA is suspended or stopped.
- Pending orders expire after their wait time. The first fill cancels that strategy's other pending orders.

**Safety changes**
- Emergency close, the daily stop, the equity floor and the profit-target close now act on every EA and mix position.
- `CloseOpenPositionSafely` now closes all positions, not just the first one found (guard audit C3).

**Port check** (`research/mix_port.py`, `tests/test_mix_port.py`)
- A Python copy of the MQL5 logic, run bar by bar, gives exactly the research setups.
- On 2 years of real H2/H4 gold with a window of 2,000+ bars: fresh level 300/300, EMA9 360/360, QM 376/387.
- The QM differences only appear with full history: multi-year patterns with stops about $2,000 away, which the sizing would skip anyway. The EA uses 3,000 bars (`inp_MixBars`).

**Not compiled.** MT5's H4 bars follow broker server time, while the research used UTC days, so H4 bar edges can differ by a few hours.
