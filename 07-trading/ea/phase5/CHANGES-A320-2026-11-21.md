## 2. MSNR, added where the data supports it

New file: `Intelligence/CMSNREngine.mqh`. It ports `07-trading/src/msnr.py`:
- Levels come from closes on H4, as key levels with k = 2, and are tracked on H1 for fresh, touch and flip (RBS/SBR).
- The weekly storyline is included.
- It runs as Gate 8 in `CTradeScorer`.

Every MSNR part was tested on the EA. The test was `research/ea_msnr_gold.py`: 2 years of H1 gold, all 32 on/off combinations, the version chosen on the earlier part only (before 1 Sep 2025), then checked once on the later part.

| MSNR part | Input (default) | Earlier part | Later part | Kept? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nearest level on the trade side must be **fresh** | `inp_MSNR_FreshGate` (**on**) | +0.08R × 33 | **+0.23R × 36** | Yes |
| Pullback must reach the level | `inp_MSNR_LevelGate` (off) | −0.07R | +0.16R | No, worse in the earlier part |
| Weekly storyline must agree | `inp_MSNR_StoryGate` (off) | −0.21R | −0.01R | No, it hurts |
| Stop beyond the MSNR level | not in EA | −0.05R | +0.22R | No, worse in the earlier part |
| Target = next opposite level | not in EA | 0–2 trades | — | No, the level is almost always closer than 1.5R |
| Base EA (no MSNR) | — | +0.02R × 58 | +0.14R × 60 | — |

Base vs base + fresh filter, whole 2 years, $10K, 0.75% risk, $0.40 cost a trade:

| | Base | **+ MSNR fresh** |
|---|---|---|
| Trades | 118 | 69 (2.4 a month) |
| Profit | $690 | **$827** |
| Worst drop | 6.7% | **3.6%** |
| Avg R a trade | +0.08 | **+0.16** |
| The5%ers 8% reached (weekly starts) | 33% | **48%** (never failed) |
| Random entries, same exits (later part) | — | −0.03R; p = 0.02 |

Same fresh filter at 1.0% risk: $1,104, worst drop 4.8%. At 1.25%: $1,381, worst drop 6.0%. The default stays at 0.75%; raising it is your call.

The 3-trade ladder with the fresh filter made $525, less than the doc exits, so the EA keeps the doc exits.

**Honest limits**
- 32 versions were tried, and the later part has only 36 trades. That is a promising result, not a proven edge.
- The Python model of your design is not line-for-line the MQL5 scorer. The Strategy Tester run on real tick data is the real check.

## 3. A325 macro / news layer (21 Nov 2026)

Tested in `research/macro_layer_gold.py` on the 4,745 trades of the A323 mix:
- news windows: CPI, jobs report and Fed meetings, using official BLS and Fed dates
- fear: VIX and GVZ
- shocks: a GVZ jump
- the 10-year yield and the dollar index

| Layer | Earlier part | Later part | Result |
