# Stocks-in-Play 5-minute breakout · PAPER ONLY

Every weekday 16:15 IST: ranks 51 large NSE stocks by opening volume vs their last 14 sessions (the "news" signal),
shows the busiest sectors, then replays the day for the top 3: trade the first 5-minute candle's breakout in its direction,
stop at 10% of the 14-day ATR, exit 15:15, full intraday costs. Method: Zarattini, Barbon & Aziz 2024 (SSRN 4729284).
