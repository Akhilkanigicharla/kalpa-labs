# A163: the ₹10,000 bot, few trades (13 Nov 2026)

Akhil: "capital is ₹10k and trading days are very less, so do accordingly." Script: `a163_nifty_gold_10k.py`
(results `a163_results.csv`; trials T0020–T0024). Every number is after per-trade costs **and** the fixed DP charge
(₹15.34 per scrip per sell day, Zerodha), which matters on small amounts: one sell on a ₹5,000 leg costs 0.31%.
Idle cash earns 4.7%/yr (LIQUIDBEES). Nifty is the price index (no dividends), so the real NIFTYBEES leg does ~1% a year better.

**Chosen: Nifty trend leg + gold held.** Highest return per unit of fall, smallest worst year, few trades.
- Trend on gold makes it worse (−36.9% fall): gold's falls are slow and its whipsaws cost more than they save.
- Caveat: holding gold (instead of trading it) was chosen after seeing the data. 50/50 equity+gold is a standard
  diversification rule, not a tuned setting, but gold's 2024–26 run flatters every gold result. Paper first.
- Tax: gold ETF gains are slab-rate if sold within 12 months, 12.5% after (STRATEGY.md §tax). Holding gold avoids short-term tax.

**Built:** `hold` strategy in `src/strategies.py` (+ test), runner accepts it; the daily paper workflow now runs the
gold leg into `paper/track-A-gold` (buy 44 GOLDBEES once, then hold). Nifty leg = existing Track A at ₹5,000.
The same phone KILL switch (`paper/track-A/flags/KILL`) stops both. Real money only after 90 paper days, the gate, and Akhil's "go".
