# A279: 25 strategies, 25 agents, one master agent (14 Nov 2026)

Akhil: "25 trades, 25 different strategies, see which strategy works quicker; if it works increase trades;
based on the chart and market increase and decrease trades; a master agent that maintains the strategies,
with a number of agents to test and make money in that market in that time frame."

Code `src/tournament.py` (tests `tests/test_tournament.py`, 7 pass). Results `a279_summary.csv`,
`a279_agents.csv`, `a279_sensitivity.csv`. Trials T0028–T0029.

## How the bot works
- **25 strategy agents.** 10 on Nifty 50 (NIFTYBEES), 5 on Bank Nifty (BANKBEES), 3 on Nifty Next 50
  (JUNIORBEES), 4 on gold (GOLDBEES), 3 on Nasdaq 100 (MON100). Rules: trend (12-month and 6-month
  momentum), moving averages (100/150/200 days), Turtle breakouts, EMA cross, MACD, RSI(2) dips,
  Bollinger dips, 52-week high, volatility regime, buy-and-hold. All settings fixed before any test.
- **Every agent paper-trades every day**, so each one always has a live score.
- **Master agent, every Friday:** scores each agent on its last 126 days (return above cash ÷ risk,
  after charges). Score above 0.25 → it gets money in proportion to its score (max 15% per agent,
  max 40% per market). Below → benched (no money), but it keeps paper-trading and comes back when it works.
  Winners get bigger trades; when more agents score well, the bot trades more.
- **Risk agent:** account down 12% from its peak → every position halved until the fall is under 6%.
- **No-trade band:** a market is traded only when its position must move by ₹500 (5% of the account).
- Costs: every NSE delivery charge, 0.1% slippage each side, ₹15.34 DP charge per ETF sold, idle cash 4.7%/yr.

## Why it makes less money
1. **Charges.** The first run picked well (14.7%/yr before charges) but paid 17.0%/yr in charges: 25 small slices
   of ₹10,000 change positions ~200 times a year and each ETF sell costs ₹15.34 (0.15% of the account). The band fixed
   most of it, but charges are still 3%/yr against 1% for the current bot.
2. **Idle money.** Only agents with a recent good score get money and they are capped, so on average just 33% of the
   account is in the market. That is why the worst fall is so small (−6.7%) and why the return is small too.
3. **Chasing.** Scores look back 126 days; money arrives after a run has already happened.

## Verdict
The master agent does what Akhil asked (25 strategies, more money and more trades to what works, less to what
doesn't, safer in falls), but it earns **half** of what the current ₹10,000 plan earns. It does **not** replace the
current bot. It runs as a **paper track** (`paper/tournament/`, daily in the paper workflow) so Akhil can watch which
agents win in real time. Not live: live trading needs LIVE_TRADING=YES, a GO file, a GATE_PASS file, 90 paper days
and Akhil's "go".
