# A280: Quant Brain, an intraday master over 25 strategy agents (14 Nov 2026)

Akhil: "AI quant trading master whose one task is to run as many trades as it can, up to 25, that make profit on
₹10k; a quant brain that explores different markets and charts and tests intraday strategies based on market
volume, sentiment, news, fundamentals, technicals and liquidity."

Code `src/quant_brain.py` (tests `tests/test_quant_brain.py`, 5 pass). Results `a280_summary.csv`, `a280_agents.csv`.
Trial T0030.

## Setup (same as A275, so the numbers compare)
5-minute bars, 18 Aug – 13 Nov 2026 (60 trading days, Yahoo's free limit), 20 liquid Nifty 50 stocks + NIFTYBEES +
BANKBEES. ₹10,000 at 5x = ₹50,000 of positions. Entry at the next bar's open + 0.02% slippage; stop assumed first if a
bar hits both; all closed by 15:15; every NSE intraday charge.

## What each input means here
| Akhil's input | How the brain uses it |
|---|---|
| Technicals | EMA 9/21, VWAP, RSI(2)/(14), opening range, Bollinger squeeze, day high/low |
| Volume | bar volume vs its 20-bar average; strongest volume wins when several stocks signal |
| Liquidity | skips bars that move more than 1.5% (thin or shocked); large-cap stocks only |
| Sentiment | breadth (share of the 22 symbols above VWAP) and Nifty vs VWAP: longs only in an up market, shorts only in a down one |
| News | no free archive of Indian intraday news (GDELT is unreachable from our cloud), so the measurable footprint of overnight news is used: the opening gap (gap-and-go, gap-fade) |
| Fundamentals | change once a quarter, so they cannot time 5-minute trades; they choose the universe (large, liquid Nifty 50 companies). `src/fundamentals.py` scores a stock from its filings |

**25 agents:** 10 strategy families (EMA+VWAP, VWAP snap-back, volume spike, opening-range breakout, RSI(2), gap-and-go,
gap-fade, breadth, squeeze breakout, relative strength) × 2–3 target/stop pairs. Each paper-trades every day.

**Master brain, every morning, using past days only:** score = each agent's net rupees after charges over the last 10
days (at least 5 trades). Positive score → money in proportion (max 50% per agent). The account takes those agents'
trades at that share of ₹50,000, **at most 25 trades a day**, and stops for the day at **−₹300** (3% of the account).
No agent with a positive score = no trades that day.

## Result (days 11–60, never seen when scoring)
| Line | Trades/day | Success rate | Charges | Net | Net/day | Worst day | Green days |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Quant Brain master** | 3.1 | 33.1% | ₹2,506 | **−₹3,154** | **−₹63** | −₹409 | 30% |
| All 25 agents, each on its own ₹50,000 | 72.7 | 32.9% | ₹1,89,826 | −₹2,43,316 | −₹4,866 | −₹14,928 | 18% |
| Best single agent, picked **after** the fact (RSI(2), 1.0%/0.5%) | 1.8 | 47.7% | ₹4,597 | +₹654 | +₹13 | −₹1,070 | 52% |

- **24 of 25 agents lost money.** 21 of them lost even **before** charges (gross below zero), so the signals have no edge
  on these 60 days; charges then make it much worse (₹11,000–₹19,000 per agent).
- The master did its job: it cut trades from 72 a day to 3, sat out 10 of 50 days, and lost ₹63 a day instead of the
  ₹513–₹772 a day of A275's 10-trade runs. It **never reached 25 trades** because no 25 agents were winning at once.
- The one green agent made ₹13 a day, and it was found only by looking back. With 25 agents, one green by luck is
  expected; 60 days is too short to tell luck from skill.

## Verdict
Rejected as a money bot: after charges no intraday strategy here makes money at ₹10,000, the same finding as A275 and
`aggressive-5k-2026-11-13.md` (306 combos). The brain runs as a **paper track** (`paper/quant-brain/`, daily after the
close) so that if an agent starts winning for real, it shows up with proof. It will trade more only when agents keep
winning. Live trading needs LIVE_TRADING=YES, GO + GATE_PASS files, 90 paper days and Akhil's "go".
