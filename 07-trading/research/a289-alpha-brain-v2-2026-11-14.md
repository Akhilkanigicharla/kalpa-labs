## Design (what pro momentum managers do)
- **Evidence for 20%:** NSE's whitepaper puts the Nifty200 Momentum 30 index at **19.19% a year, Apr 2005 – Feb 2026**
  (parent index 15.08%). Momentum is the only Indian factor with a ~20% long-run record. Its last 5 years were weaker
  (about 11% a year, sharpely.in).
- **Stock sleeve:** rank stocks every month by 12-1 momentum or NSE's normalised momentum score (6- and 12-month return ÷
  risk); must be up over 12 months; whole shares must be affordable on ₹10,000; keep a holding while it stays in the top
  2K (buffer, cuts trading); when Nifty is below its 200-day average, move to the best of gold / Nasdaq ETF, or cash.
- **ETF sleeve (dual momentum, Antonacci):** best 2 of Nifty, Next 50, Bank, PSU banks, infra, CPSE, gold, Nasdaq 100,
  Hang Seng, only if they beat the liquid fund.
- 64 settings fixed before testing, **chosen on 2011–2018 only**, then run untouched on 2019 – 13 Nov 2026.

## The bias check that breaks it
The stock lists are **today's** Nifty 50 and Next 50, so they hold the companies that already won (HAL, Mazagon Dock,
CG Power, IRFC, Polycab…). Two checks:
| Same chosen rule, 2019–26 | Return/yr | Worst fall |
|---|---|---|
| Next 50 names listed after 2012 removed | **9.0%** | −41.1% |
| The 10 best-performing Next 50 names removed | **10.5%** | −38.7% |

**Most of the 25.9% is survivorship bias.** An honest estimate for this design today: about 10–15% a year with −30% to
−40% falls. That is no better than the current bot, and riskier.

## Verdict and the professional next step
- No design tested here makes 20% a year honestly on ₹10,000. The best real record (NSE's momentum index) is ~19% over
  20 years, but only ~11% over the last 5, with deep falls.
- What a professional quant does next: rebuild the universe **point-in-time** (which stocks were in Nifty 100 on each
  past date, from NSE's index-change notices) and re-test. Only that number can be trusted. Roadmap A290.
- Until then the current bot stays the ₹10,000 plan; nothing goes live.
