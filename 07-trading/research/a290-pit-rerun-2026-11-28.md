# A290: honest index lists, and A289 without survivorship bias (28 Nov 2026)

**Answer:** with the stocks that were really in the index each month, A289's chosen rule earns **9.5% a year** on
2019 – 27 Nov 2026 (worst fall −25.8%), not 25.9–30%. Across all 64 settings the median is **12.4%**, and only 1 in 64
reaches 20%. Paper only; nothing changes in the money bot.

## 1. The membership files (new)
`07-trading/research/index_membership/` (built by `07-trading/src/index_membership.py`, cloud only):

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `n50_monthly.csv`, `n100_monthly.csv`, `n200_monthly.csv` | every month-end Jan 2011 – Nov 2026: count + member symbols |
| `events.csv` | 1,500+ index changes (date, index, in/out, company, symbol, link to the NSE release) |
| `*_anomalies.txt` | problems found while rebuilding (all empty) |

How: all equity press releases on niftyindices.com/press-release (NSE Indices' own archive, PDFs from 2010 on; scanned
ones read with tesseract OCR) are parsed into changes. Starting from NSE's constituent CSVs of 28 Nov 2026 the script
walks back in time, undoing each change. Old releases without symbols are matched by name using NSE's EQUITY_L,
namechange and symbolchange files. A release without its own Nifty 100 section is derived from Nifty 50 + Next 50.

Checks (`pytest tests/test_index_membership.py`, 9 pass):
- Nifty 100 has **100 members in every month**, 101 from Apr 2016 to May 2020 (Tata Motors DVR; NSE's release
  ind_prs22022016_2 says "the Nifty 100 index shall have 101 securities"). Nifty 50: 50 (51 while DVR was in).
  Nifty 200: 199–201.
- Walking back never removes a stock that was not there, or adds one already there (0 anomalies in all 3 indices).
- Fixed by hand, each from NSE's own follow-up release: the Mar 2020 review was deferred (ind_prs23032020) and redone
  for 26 Jun 2020; Yes Bank was moved up to 19 Mar 2020; Nov 2013 date moved to 18 Nov (holiday); two revocation
  tables (IREDA Mar 2024, Vodafone Idea / Central Bank Sep 2024, Nifty 200 only).
- Before Apr 2016, NSE's own releases show a few Nifty 100 stocks outside CNX 200 (e.g. Torrent Power 2011–13,
  Bajaj Holdings 2014–16); after the 2016 method change Nifty 100 is always inside Nifty 200. Kept as published.

## 2. A289 re-run (`research/a290_pit_rerun.py`, results `a290_grid.csv`)
Same 64 settings, same ₹10,000 whole-share engine with every NSE charge, same rule: chosen on 2011–2018 only (worst

(The biased column is 30.2% here vs 25.9% in A289 because the list is NSE's 28 Nov CSV and prices are fresh.)

Reading: the A289 estimate "about 10–15% a year" was right. The point-in-time pick (17.7%) came with a −33% fall, worse
than the −30% limit used to choose it on 2011–18, so it is weak evidence, not a new strategy. Best on 2019–26 in
hindsight (not usable for choosing): Nifty 100, top 3, NSE score, cash when Nifty is weak, 21.8%/yr, −22.0%. NSE's
risk-adjusted score beat plain 12-1 momentum in most bias-free settings; that is a lead for a fresh out-of-sample
test, not proof. The current bot in A289 made 13.1%/yr with a −15.4% worst fall.

## 3. Limits
- Yahoo has no prices for companies that later delisted (HDFC, Cairn, Ranbaxy, …). Share of point-in-time member-months
  with prices (`a290_coverage.csv`): Nifty 100 89–95% in 2011–15, 97–100% in 2019–26 (the test years).
- A stock whose prices stop while held is sold at its last price at the next month-end (a merger usually paid in shares).
- Monthly list = list at the calendar month-end; NSE announces changes 2–4 weeks ahead, so this uses no hidden future.
- One data source for prices (Yahoo via yfinance). No tips; this trades nothing.

Run again: `python3 07-trading/src/index_membership.py` (≈2 min, refreshes from NSE) then
`python3 07-trading/research/a290_pit_rerun.py` (≈8 min).
