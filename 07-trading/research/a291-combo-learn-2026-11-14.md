# A291: learn from the winning trades of Alpha Brain, the current bot and Nifty, then re-apply (14 Nov 2026)

Akhil: "Combine successful trades from alpha brain and current bot and nifty, analyse the success trades and reapply the
same strategy and give me results again."
Script `research/a291_combo_learn.py`. Files: `a291_trades.csv` (2,552 monthly trades), `a291_success_buckets.csv`,
`a291_rules.txt`, `a291_results.csv`, `a291_rolling.csv`, `a291_daily_2026.csv`. Trial T0036.

## Method
- Every month, each thing the three strategies would buy is one trade held for a month: Alpha Brain's top 10 Nifty 50
  momentum stocks, the bot's Nifty ETF (when Nifty's trend is up) and gold ETF, Nifty buy-and-hold, and the Nasdaq ETF.
- A trade is a success if it made more than 1% in the month (pays the charges).
- **Learned only from 2011–2018 trades**, then re-applied untouched to 2019 – 13 Nov 2026 with the ₹10,000 engine.

## What the 2011–18 winners had in common
| Source | Trades | Success | Avg month |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alpha Brain stocks | 1,030 | 55.0% | +2.1% |
| Nasdaq ETF | 94 | 55.3% | +1.8% |
| Nifty (hold) | 29 | 55.2% | +1.6% |
| Bot: Nifty in uptrend | 74 | 43.2% | +0.5% |
| Bot: gold | 103 | 41.7% | +0.5% |

The learned rule (`a291_rules.txt`): buy when 12-1 momentum is above 4%, and the fear gauge (India VIX) is not at
its calmest; when fear is high, only buy if the last 3 months were quiet.

## Re-applied on 2019 – Nov 2026 (₹10,000)
| Line | Return/yr | Worst fall | ₹10,000 became |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Combo learned from 2011–18 winners** | **1.9%** | −40.8% | ₹11,612 |
| Alpha Brain v2 (Nifty 50 version) | 15.5% | −27.9% | ₹31,087 |
| Current bot | 13.4% | −15.3% | ₹26,950 |
| Buy and hold Nifty | 12.6% | −36.2% | ₹25,418 |
| *Hindsight combo, learned on ALL years incl. 2019–26 (not usable)* | *24.4%* | *−14.9%* | *₹55,770* |

- On 2019–26 the trades the rule picked won 48.8% of the time, against 51.7% for all candidates. The patterns of past
  winners did not repeat.
- Every 12-month start since 2019: combo median +2.4%, lost money 43% of the time; current bot median +15.8%, lost 4%.
- 2026 so far: combo ₹9,434 (worst fall −17.1%).

## Verdict
Copying what past winners looked like works only when you already know the answer (24.4% with hindsight, 1.9% without).
Rejected. The current bot stays the ₹10,000 plan; Alpha Brain v2 (Nifty 50 version) stays the best honest
higher-return candidate, pending the point-in-time re-test (A290).
