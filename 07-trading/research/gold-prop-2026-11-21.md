## 3. Can a gold strategy pass these rules? (`research/gold_prop.py`, report `reports/gold-prop-2026-11-20.md`)
Gold in ₹ per gram from COMEX × USD/INR; 1-gram Petal lots; 0.04% costs; long and short; a fresh challenge every 5th trading day 2016-11 → 2026-11; strategies fixed before any result.
- **Plain edge, 2016–26:** daily trend +0.47%/trade (133 trades, 26% wins). Donchian ≈ 0. RSI-2 reversion ≈ 0. Random ≈ +0.03%.
- **Challenge outcome:** every rule set × strategy × risk level (0.5–3%) has a **negative expected ₹ per attempt**.
  - Best pass rate: trend at 1% risk, Hindustan, 26% (31% fail), but a median of **~470 trading days** to pass.
  - At 2–3% risk, 82–92% of attempts fail, and random entries do about as well.
- **Why:** gold's real edge is a slow daily trend (~2% a year at 1% risk). A 10% target in reasonable time needs risk that the 5% daily / 10% max-loss rules punish.
- Matches the industry data: ~14% of challenge buyers pass and ~7% ever get paid (FPFX Tech, 300,000 accounts).
