# Kronos (video: "Open Source Kronos AI: Candlestick Trading Model Tested in AI Agents", David Arias CFA), 13 Nov 2026

- What it is: a foundation model for candlestick (K-line) data. Paper: Shi et al., "Kronos: A Foundation Model for the Language
  of Financial Markets", arXiv 2508.02739 (2 Aug 2025), also AAAI. Pre-trained on 12 billion+ K-line records from 45 exchanges.
  Claims are forecasting metrics (RankIC +93% vs the best time-series foundation model, 9% lower volatility MAE), not trading
  profit after costs.
- Code: github.com/shiyu-coder/Kronos, MIT, commit 67b630e (13 Apr 2026). Models on Hugging Face (NeoQuasar): mini 4.1M (context 2048),
  small 24.7M, base 102.3M (context 512); large 499M not released. Weights load as safetensors via huggingface_hub; model code
  reviewed (no network, shell, eval or pickle loading).
- Leakage risk: Nifty is very likely inside its pre-training data, so any test must use data AFTER Aug 2025.
- Planned test (rules fixed in advance, `/home/claude/kronos-test/daily.py`): daily Nifty from 1 Aug 2025, lookback 400, predict
  next close (10 samples), hold NIFTYBEES only when it predicts a rise, else liquid ETF; then 5-min NIFTYBEES, trade only when the
  predicted move beats the 0.106% round-trip cost. Compare with "always up" accuracy and with holding Nifty.
