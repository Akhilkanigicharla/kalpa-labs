## What the bot does now
`paper-trade.yml` runs `python -m src.msnr --paper paper/msnr` every weekday at 16:15 IST.
- It replays only the setups whose latest report says PASS.
- It writes `paper/msnr/ledger.csv`, `state.json` and `latest.txt`.
- It risks ₹50 per trade (1% of ₹5,000) and obeys the Track-A KILL file.
- Paper only: no broker, no keys.

Before any real money: 90 days of paper, then the gate (C12 thresholds) and Akhil's yes.

## Who created MSNR? (searched 20 Nov 2026)
**No single inventor is on record.** What the sources say:
- **A Malaysian trader-blogger (Zezu Zaza):** the method started around 2005–2007 in the Malaysian forex community, shared online through "Cari Gold Wang Siber", by low-profile traders he does not name. He started using it in 2008. (zezuzaza.com)
- **An Indonesian broker blog:** there is no single guru who claims it. It grew from forum to forum. (foreximf.com)
- **Who made it popular abroad:** teachers known as Alchemist, ZakFX, EMS Trinity, "MSNR Trading Secret", Udara and others. These are the authors that the open-source TradingView scripts were built from.
  - One post says Alchemist never claimed to own it.
  - The same post says Alchemist learned ranges from ICT and added MSNR around 2023. (X, @UrwatuSuleiman, Mar 2025)
- **Quasimodo (QM):** its origin is unknown; the name is thought to come from the hunchback's shape. (investinglive.com)
- **Sujith Surya** (Akhil's playlist) teaches it. He did not create it.
- **Grokipedia's "early 2020s" date** clashes with the Malaysian source, so it isn't used.

## Independent study (second check)
Sean Solano Paul (Bengaluru, 2026; code MIT, Zenodo doi 10.5281/zenodo.23537688; github.com/Bruhmoment2023-real/malaysian-snr) codified MSNR and tested it on crypto, gold and silver against matched random entries:
- **Before costs:** −0.058R per trade vs −0.060R for random entries (z = 0.26), so no edge.
- **After costs:** about −0.55R per trade.
- **Parameter search:** no setting with 100+ trades survived false-discovery control.

QM, the only setup that worked on the NSE ETFs, does not work on gold anywhere.

**Reading:** the results change with market, timeframe and direction, and the independent study found no edge. Gold-specific MSNR is **not** added to the bot.
- The pooled-ETF paper run (which includes GOLDBEES) continues as a 90-day paper test only.
- Avg R and PF can disagree when a gap fill lands next to the stop (tiny risk, huge R). In that case PF (money) is the safer number.
