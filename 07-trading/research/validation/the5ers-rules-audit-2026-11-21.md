# The5%ers High Stakes $10K: rules audit vs EA_XAUUSD (2026-11-21)

Auditor: Claude (compliance-audit sub-agent). Scope: The5%ers High Stakes 2-step, $10K, MT5. EA folder: `07-trading/ea/phase5/EA_XAUUSD/`.
Method: (1) official the5ers.com pages, fetched live today (page HTML + embedded FAQ data); (2) independent 2026 secondary sources; (3) conflicts reconciled below. Nothing was logged into or signed up for. Quotes are short extracts.

**Status labels.** CONFIRMED means an official the5ers.com page fetched today says it and at least one second source agrees (or a second official page agrees). OFFICIAL-ONLY means only The5%ers' own pages say it. UNVERIFIED means no official page confirms it.

## 0. Headline findings

1. **The day's loss base is wrong in the EA (BREACH-RISK).** The5%ers uses the **higher of balance or equity at 00:00 server time**. The EA uses only equity at its first check after rollover. If a trade carries a floating loss over midnight, the EA's 4% stop can sit **below** The5%ers' 5% line. Example: $10,000 balance and $9,800 equity at midnight. The5%ers breaches at $9,500. The EA stops at $9,408. The account is terminated before the EA acts.
2. **The T&C require prior written approval for any automated trading software (BREACH-RISK, contractual).** The FAQ says "any EA" is fine, but the binding T&C require written approval first. Akhil must email The5%ers and get written approval before the EA trades the account.
3. **Bulk trading and simultaneous opposite positions (BREACH-RISK, ban; depends on how The5%ers reads it).** The 12-strategy mix plus the main strategy can hold many XAUUSD positions at once, including long and short together. "Bulk trading" is on the official prohibited list.
4. **News window (MEDIUM).** The EA's 120-minute blackout is much wider than the 2-minute rule. But it fails open when the MT5 calendar returns nothing. It also uses MT5's "high" importance, while The5%ers uses Forex Factory red folders.
5. **The $10K account itself checks out.** The 5% daily loss, 10% static max loss, Step 1 target (10% New / 8% Classic), Step 2 target of 5%, 3 profitable days at 0.5%, and 1:100 leverage are all confirmed. Metals are 1:25 margin, not 1:100. The fee is **$78 Classic / $69 New**.
