## How this world works
1. **Pick from real revenue data, not guesses.** TrustMRR public AI endpoints only (`https://trustmrr.com/api/ai/discovery`, `/startup/{slug}.md`); its `llms.txt` says do not scrape the site. Every idea needs 2 sources (CLAUDE.md fact rule).
2. **One problem per app.** Small enough to build v1 in under 2 weeks with free tools (Netlify / Cloudflare free, Supabase free, free models).
3. **Users from where people already look:** app stores (Chrome Web Store, Google Workspace Marketplace, Play Store), Google search pages, one evergreen YouTube demo per app, Facebook groups, short videos.
4. **Proof before scale.** First signal = free sign-ups and real use. No payments until delivery is proven and Akhil says go (18 Oct rule). Keep apps that earn, retire the rest.
5. Same rules as every world: engineering checklist, 20 SaaS UX patterns, 20-item SEO list, DPDP privacy, show Akhil before anything goes live.
